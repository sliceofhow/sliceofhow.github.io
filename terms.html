3. How our videos are made

Our videos are produced with the help of AI and computer graphics:

  • Narration is a synthetic, computer-generated voice (text-to-speech), not a recording of a person.
  • Visuals are mostly 3D cutaway illustrations rendered by computer: simplified, stylized models made to explain a principle. They are not exact plans and may not be to scale. Some videos also use animated data charts, text cards and licensed stock footage or photos (Pexels, Pixabay), which are credited in the video description.
  • AI-generated scenes: some videos also include AI-generated images or clips that reconstruct a scene for context. They are illustrations, not real footage, and videos that contain them carry YouTube's altered or synthetic content disclosure.
  • Scripts are drafted with the help of AI language models, from facts gathered from public sources, and are checked against those facts by an automated review before upload. The main sources are listed in each video's description.

We work to get the facts right, but mistakes can happen. If you spot one, please tell us (section 8).
